Robust to Unseen Viewpoints

One unified model. At test time the robot sees only the wrist camera and one external camera, while the other external camera is blind with its calibration kept. In training, each external camera covered only one half of the table. At test time the visible camera is moved to unseen poses, including the half that only the other camera covered in training.

Real world · Streaming

Faster Execution with Fewer Denoising Steps

Both videos play in real time and use the same accelerated pipeline, a fixed-layout KV cache with resynchronization. They differ only in the number of denoising steps per action chunk, 25/50 (video/action) on the left and 10/20 on the right. With fewer steps, the robot waits less between action chunks.

Cameras All Around the Table

Each video shows our model performing the same task with the external camera rotated around the table by 0° to 180° in azimuth from the canonical view.

Simulation · Partial observation

Imagining the Blind Camera's View

When an external camera is removed, the model imagines its view from the remaining cameras and their relative geometry, and predicts actions from visible view together with this imagined view. On LIBERO-Long, the success rate is 92.8% with one visible external camera and 92.4% with two visible external cameras.
